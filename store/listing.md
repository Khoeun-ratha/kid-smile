# Google Play store listing — Kid Smile

Copy each field into **Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store
listing**. Add Khmer as a second language under **Manage translations**.

## English (en-US) — default language

**App name** (max 30)
```
Kid Smile: Fun Kids Quiz
```

**Short description** (max 80)
```
Fun offline quizzes for kids in English and Khmer. No ads, no sign-up!
```

**Full description** (max 4000)
```
Kid Smile turns learning into play! 😄

Children answer bright, friendly quiz questions about the world around them – with big buttons, colorful pictures and happy cheers for every answer.

🐶 5 FUN TOPICS
• Animals – sounds, homes, legs and more
• Colors – what color is the sun, the grass, a banana?
• Numbers – counting and simple math
• Shapes – circles, squares, triangles and stars
• Nature – weather, plants, the sky and seasons
• Mixed Quiz – a little of everything!

🇰🇭 ENGLISH OR KHMER
Play in English or in Khmer (ខ្មែរ). Switch any time in Settings – great for learning both languages.

✈️ WORKS 100% OFFLINE
More than 1,000 questions are built in. No Wi-Fi or mobile data needed – perfect for car trips, waiting rooms and anywhere else.

⏱️ SHORT, KID-SIZED ROUNDS
Choose 6, 8 or 10 questions. A friendly timer keeps the game moving, and kids see the right answer straight away, so every mistake becomes a chance to learn.

⭐ STARS & PROGRESS
Earn up to 3 stars per round, review the answers, and watch your best score grow in "My Progress".

👨‍👩‍👧 SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts or sign-up
• No personal data collected – ever

Kid Smile is made for children aged about 4 to 8. Have fun, learn something new, and keep smiling!
```
