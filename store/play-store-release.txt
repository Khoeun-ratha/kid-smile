Kid Smile turns learning into play! 😄

Children answer bright, friendly quiz questions about the world around them – with big buttons, colorful pictures and happy cheers for every answer.

🐶 5 FUN TOPICS
• Animals – sounds, homes, legs and more
• Colors – what color is the sun, the grass, a banana?
• Numbers – counting and simple math
• Shapes – circles, squares, triangles and stars
• Nature – weather, plants, the sky and seasons
• Mixed Quiz – a little of everything!

🇰🇭 ENGLISH OR KHMER
Play in English or in Khmer (ខ្មែរ). Switch any time in Settings – great for learning both languages.

✈️ WORKS 100% OFFLINE
More than 1,000 questions are built in. No Wi-Fi or mobile data needed – perfect for car trips, waiting rooms and anywhere else.

⏱️ SHORT, KID-SIZED ROUNDS
Choose 6, 8 or 10 questions. A friendly timer keeps the game moving, and kids see the right answer straight away, so every mistake becomes a chance to learn.

⭐ STARS & PROGRESS
Earn up to 3 stars per round, review the answers, and watch your best score grow in "My Progress".

👨‍👩‍👧 SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts or sign-up
• No personal data collected – ever

Kid Smile is made for children aged about 4 to 8. Have fun, learn something new, and keep smiling!

 6. STEP BY STEP
========================================================================

BEFORE YOU START
  [ ] Back up D:\Mobile\kid_smile\release-keys\ (upload-keystore.jks and
      key.properties.backup, which holds the password) to a USB drive or
      private cloud. Never share it or put it in GitHub. You need it to
      publish every future update.
  [ ] Create a developer account: https://play.google.com/console
      (one-time US$25, ID verification can take a few days)

CREATE THE APP
  [ ] Play Console -> Create app -> fill in section 2 above
  [ ] Fill in all forms in section 5
  [ ] Grow -> Store presence -> Main store listing -> sections 1, 3, 4

CLOSED TEST (required for new personal accounts)
  [ ] Testing -> Closed testing -> Create track
  [ ] Add at least 12 testers (their Gmail addresses or a Google Group)
  [ ] Create release -> upload app-release.aab -> release name: 1.0.0
  [ ] Accept "Play App Signing" when asked (recommended)
  [ ] Send testers the opt-in link; they install and keep it 14 days

PRODUCTION
  [ ] After 14 days with 12+ testers: Dashboard -> Apply for production
  [ ] Production -> Create release -> upload the bundle -> Send for review
  [ ] Review usually takes a few days (longer for kids apps)

 8. PUBLISHING AN UPDATE LATER
========================================================================

In PowerShell, from D:\Mobile\kid_smile:

      .\store\build-release.ps1 -Bump

This raises the version (1.0.0+1 -> 1.0.1+2), builds the signed bundle and
copies it to your Desktop as kid-smile-<version>.aab. Upload that file in
Play Console -> Production -> Create release, then commit pubspec.yaml.

On a new computer: copy key.properties.backup from the key folder to
D:\Mobile\kid_smile\mobile\android\key.properties and update storeFile
if the keystore is in a different place.
