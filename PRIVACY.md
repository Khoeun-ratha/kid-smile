# Kid Smile — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 October 2026_

Kid Smile is a quiz game for young children. We built it to be safe for
kids: **it does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal
information.**

## What the app collects

**Nothing.** Kid Smile has:

- no sign-up, accounts, or logins
- no advertising
- no analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools
- no in-app purchases
- no access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or photos

## What stays on the device

To work, the app saves a few things **only on the phone or tablet it is
installed on**:

- the quiz questions that come with the app
- the chosen language (English or Khmer)
- quiz scores, so the child can see their progress

This information never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing
its data in the device settings, deletes all of it.

## Internet

The version of Kid Smile on Google Play works completely offline and does
not send or receive any data over the internet. All questions are included
in the app.

## Children's privacy

Kid Smile is designed for children and follows Google Play's Families
Policy. Because we collect no personal information at all, there is
nothing for a parent to review or delete on our side.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version adds online
features), we will update this page and the date above before releasing
that version.
